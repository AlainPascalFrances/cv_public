Profile

Hydrogeologist at LNEG, Portugal’s National Laboratory of Energy and Geology, with more than 25 years of experience in Hydrogeology and Geoscientific Information Management. Combines numerical groundwater modelling (MODFLOW 6, FloPy, PEST) and coupled unsaturated–saturated zone modelling (MARMITES, of which he is the developer) with hydrogeophysics (ERT, TDEM/FDEM, MRS), remote sensing and GIS.

Currently involved in:

  • The EU project DRYAD (https://dryad-project.eu/), from which he is the leader of Work Package 3 (modelling of Mediterranean agro-silvo-pastoral ecosystems)

  • The update of the Hydrogeological Map of Mozambique (DNGRH/World Bank) Capacity building through PanAfGeo (https://panafgeo.eurogeosurveys.org/)
